# ICAR & KVK Agricultural Advisory Guidelines

## 1. Rice (Paddy) Cultivation & Disease Management
- **Sowing Window:** For Kharif season, sow nursery between May 25 and June 15. Transplant 21-25 day old seedlings by July 15.
- **Water Management:** Maintain 2-3 cm shallow water during initial transplanting, increasing to 5 cm during tillering. Alternate wetting and drying (AWD) saves 25% irrigation water.
- **Pest & Disease Control:**
  - **Rice Blast (*Magnaporthe oryzae*):** Spindle-shaped lesions with grey center. Spray Tricyclazole 75 WP @ 0.6 g/L or Azoxystrobin 23 SC @ 1 ml/L. Avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer.
  - **Brown Planthopper (BPH):** Form alleyways of 30 cm every 2-3 meters. Spray Pymetrozine 50 WG @ 120 g/acre or Triflumezopyrim 10 SC @ 94 ml/acre at base of hill.
  - **Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB):** Drain excess field water, reduce top-dressed nitrogen, spray Copper Oxychloride 50 WP @ 2.5 g/L with Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L.

## 2. Cotton Pest Management & Agronomy
- **Sowing & Spacing:** Sow during onset of monsoon (June-July). Spacing: 90 cm × 60 cm for hybrid varieties.
- **Pink Bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella*):**
  - Install yellow sticky traps and pheromone traps @ 5 per acre for monitoring.
  - If rosette flowers exceed 5%, spray Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG @ 5 g/10 L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 3 ml/10 L of water.
- **Sucking Pests (Aphids, Jassids, Whiteflies):** Spray Flonicamid 50 WG @ 60 g/acre or Diafenthiuron 50 WP @ 200 g/acre. Encourage ladybird beetles and chrysoperla predators.

## 3. Maize (Corn) Production Technology
- **Sowing Window:** Kharif: June 15 to July 15. Rabi: October 15 to November 15.
- **Fall Armyworm (*Spodoptera frugiperda*):**
  - Monitor central whorl for pin-hole leaf damage and sawdust-like frass.
  - Early stage: Spray *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt) @ 2 g/L or Azadirachtin 1500 ppm @ 5 ml/L.
  - Severe infestation: Whorl application of Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 0.5 ml/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.4 ml/L.

## 4. Wheat Rust & Weed Control
- **Sowing Schedule:** Optimal sowing between November 1 and November 20 for timely sown irrigated wheat.
- **Yellow / Brown Rust (*Puccinia striiformis*):**
  - At first appearance of yellow pustules in linear rows on leaves, spray Propiconazole 25 EC (Tilt) @ 1 ml/L or Tebuconazole 25.9 EC @ 1 ml/L.
- **Weed Management:** For *Phalaris minor* (Gulli danda), apply Clodinafop-propargyl 15 WP @ 160 g/acre at 30-35 days after sowing.

## 5. Pulses (Chickpea & Pigeonpea)
- **Seed Treatment:** Treat seeds with *Rhizobium* culture and *Trichoderma viride* @ 4 g/kg seed to prevent wilt and root rot.
- **Pod Borer (*Helicoverpa armigera*):** Set up bird perches @ 20 per acre. Spray Indoxacarb 14.5 SC @ 1 ml/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/L at 50% flowering stage.

## 6. Soil Health & Organic Amendments
- **Green Manuring:** Grow and incorporate *Dhaincha* (*Sesbania aculeata*) or Sunnhemp at 45 days stage before Kharif paddy to add 60-80 kg organic Nitrogen per hectare.
- **Biofertilizers:** Apply Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 2 kg/acre mixed with 200 kg farmyard manure (FYM) to solubilize fixed soil phosphorus.
